Admission Requirements

Students applying to Kiethston Academy should provide the following documents:

  • Completed application form
  • Valid identification document
  • Previous academic results
  • Relevant school-leaving certificate
  • Programme-specific requirements
  • Proof of residence where applicable

Please note that requirements may vary depending on the programme.
